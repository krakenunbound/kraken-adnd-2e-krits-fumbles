# Changelog: Kraken's Krits & Fumbles 2E Automation

## 2.0.0 (2026-10-23)

- Every row of the module's ten weapon tables now does its part in FGU (was about a dozen phrases): extra and self damage are rolled and applied, maximum damage and the extra weapon die change the next damage roll, lost attacks, half move, no shield AC, acts last and bleeding go on the tracker; the rest is a note for the DM.
- Player rolls work too (1.0 only handled the DM's own rolls).
- Options group Kraken Krits & Fumbles: chance for crits and fumbles (default 1 in 3, as 1.0), apply or chat only, PCs only, GM-only results.
- No module needed for the automation; the module stays the book to read. 2E's own HRFC option is left to the ruleset when ours is off.

## 1.0.0

First version: d6 gate and the module's weapon tables, a few phrases applied as effects.
